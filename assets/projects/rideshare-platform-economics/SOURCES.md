# Sources

Charts copied unchanged from [hql7-luo/rideshare-platform-economics](https://github.com/hql7-luo/rideshare-platform-economics/tree/3dbb1792dc15b1d1217c75bee50668d15ac5669c/charts) at commit `3dbb179` (generated by `src/make_charts.py`).

Data: NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission High Volume For-Hire Vehicle trip records, February 2019 to August 2026 (91 monthly files), plus the official taxi zone lookup. Uber is license HV0003 and Lyft HV0005. Gross take is base passenger fare minus driver pay; it is not profit. Outside checks: TLC's February 2026 license review report (December 2025 trip count) and the UCLA Labor Center's 2023 HVFHV analysis. Source notes: [docs/sources.md](https://github.com/hql7-luo/rideshare-platform-economics/blob/main/docs/sources.md). The congestion-zone comparison is descriptive, not a causal estimate.

Independent project · Oct 2026

Marketing Experimentation & Growth Strategy

I used data from a real experiment with 64,000 shoppers to find out which promotional emails actually increase purchases—and whether smarter targeting is worth the extra effort.

Problem
A retailer needs to know whether an email creates purchases that would not otherwise happen, which apparel campaign to use, and whether more detailed customer selection is worth the extra work.
What I did
I used SQL and Python to analyze a real 2008 experiment with 64,000 shoppers: compare three email options, study customer differences, and weigh email-cost and sending-limit scenarios.
Takeaway
The men’s apparel email had the highest purchase rate. A more complex recipient strategy did not prove a reliable advantage over random selection sending the same number of emails.

01 · EVIDENCE

Observed sales and unavailable hours

Aligned daily panels use different units. Daily exposure = unavailable hours / (200 series × 16 operating hours, 06:00–22:00). These are observed sales; unavailable hours may conceal demand, but the data does not quantify lost sales.

02 · ACTION

03 · VALIDATION

Compare actual sales with the holdout forecast

Three rolling validation folds inside the first 90 days select each model before the untouched June 26–July 2 holdout. Dashed predictions are scored; dotted July 3–9 forecasts have no observed outcomes. These models predict recorded sales, not latent demand.

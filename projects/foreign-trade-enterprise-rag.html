Problem

Relevant is not the same as authoritative.

A catalog, an SOP and an old quotation can all be relevant to the same question—and disagree. Employees need to distinguish current policy from historical terms, new proposals and fields that have never been verified.

The design problem was not just finding information. It was making the authority, origin and limits of each answer visible.

System

A workflow for knowledge you can inspect.

01

Retrieve

Exact SKU / product ID lookup, dense retrieval and BM25, combined with RRF.

02

Ground

Each claim points to evidence. Missing fields stay unverified; historical terms stay dated.

03

Govern

Review pending proposals before approval or rejection. Retain superseded facts in history.

04

Update

Approval updates Product Master. Controlled reindexing updates future answers and citations.

The public answerer is local and extractive; no answer-generating LLM is enabled. Multilingual MiniLM via FastEmbed / ONNX is optional. The reproducible demo and reported benchmark use the non-neural deterministic hash fallback.

Knowledge governance

New information is a proposal. Not yet a fact.

In the recorded synthetic workflow, a proposed MOQ changes from 144 to 180 units. The Reviewer compares both values and their source versions, then records a decision. Pending or rejected proposals cannot overwrite current authority; approved replacements preserve the prior value and audit history.

Pending → Approved / Rejected; approved facts may later be Superseded.

Separate responsibilities, explicit permissions.

Employee

Ask questions and inspect evidence and Product Master.

Reviewer

Review proposed facts, record decisions and inspect audit history.

Admin

Stage validated sources and control ingestion and reindex jobs.

Scope & limitations

This is an internal evaluation using synthetic data and questions authored by the developer’s agent, with automated source checks. The benchmark uses deterministic hashing + BM25 / RRF. It does not establish independent human validation, neural embedding quality, or real enterprise production accuracy.

Reported failures include partial identifiers, incidental wording and historical/current scope confusion. A source-backed answer can still address the wrong question.

Evidence & outcomes

An end-to-end workflow, made reproducible.

Built hybrid retrieval with claim-level citations, role-separated approvals and an auditable Product Master. The recorded workflow ends with the employee answer citing the newly approved 180-unit MOQ after reindexing. Published v1.0.0 with documentation, a real demo recording, frozen evaluations and Docker / GitHub Actions validation.

Public data. Explicit boundaries.

All business records are synthetic: no proprietary company, customer or private quotation data. Uploads are bounded, validated, sensitivity-scanned and quarantined; browser tokens stay in memory, not localStorage. Private runtime data is excluded from Git. These are demo controls, not an independent security audit or a deployed enterprise service.

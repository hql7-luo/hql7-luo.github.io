Evidence & outcomes

Business interpretation

453 records → 299 paid-model observations
Log-price OLS, full and reduced specifications
R² = 0.575 · Adjusted R² = 0.555

Stronger capability and larger parameter counts were associated with higher prices; open-source status, faster output, and newer release years were associated with lower prices after controls. Provider effects remained important. These sample associations support model-to-task matching and price benchmarking.

✦ IS 451 Most Trendy Technology Award

Scope & limitations

These findings describe associations, not causality, within the analyzed sample of paid models.

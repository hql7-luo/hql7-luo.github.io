Business problem

The question worth solving.

Which observable model characteristics are associated with paid LLM API prices?

Approach

From problem to system.

Clean the model records → compare full and reduced log-price OLS specifications → interpret provider and model characteristics.

  1. Clean the model records
  2. compare full and reduced log-price OLS specifications
  3. interpret provider and model characteristics

Findings & recommendations

Business interpretation

453 records → 299 paid-model observations
Log-price OLS, full and reduced specifications
R² = 0.575 · Adjusted R² = 0.555

Stronger capability and larger parameter counts were associated with higher prices; open-source status, faster output, and newer release years were associated with lower prices after controls. Provider effects remained important. These sample associations support model-to-task matching and price benchmarking.

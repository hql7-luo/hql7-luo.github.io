What predicts LLM API price?

Used interpretable OLS regression to examine whether capability, model size, openness, release timing, and provider effects are associated with paid LLM API prices.

Evidence & outcomes

Business interpretation

453 raw records → 299 paid models
Log-price OLS, full and reduced specifications
R² 0.575 · Adjusted R² 0.555

Stronger capability and larger parameter counts were associated with higher prices; open-source status, faster output, and newer release years were associated with lower prices after controls. Provider effects remained important. The analysis explains associations—not causality—and supports model-to-task matching and price benchmarking.

Scope & limitations

These findings describe associations, not causality, within the analyzed sample of paid models.

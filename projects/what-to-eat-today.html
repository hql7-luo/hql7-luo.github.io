What to Eat Today

A fast, explainable meal decision product that converts preferences and recent behavior into a focused local recommendation.

Problem

The question worth solving.

Routine meal decisions consume time and repeat the same recent choices.

System

From problem to system.

Five preference questions, scored candidates, weighted selection, and history.

Inside the workflow

Yesterday is excluded; meals from 2–3 days ago are downweighted to 0.35.

Technologies & methods

Next.js / React / TypeScript / Tailwind / Zustand / Motion / Vitest

Evidence & outcomes

More about the recommendation design

92 local dishes
23 categories
5 decision inputs

The recommendation system runs locally rather than calling a third-party recommendation API. Budget, spice, dietary goal, party size, and preparation time shape the pool; favorites, exclusions, history, and preferences persist in the browser.

Scope & limitations

The evidence covers a working local product and its decision logic; no user study or behavioral outcome is claimed.

Problem

The question worth solving.

How can staffing and training decisions meet freight demand while reducing modeled workforce cost?

System

From problem to system.

Translate freight demand into capacity requirements → optimize hiring and training under service constraints → inform future staffing scenarios with forecasts.

Technologies & methods

Workforce optimization / Exponential smoothing

Evidence & outcomes

Model and decision

~15% lower modeled annual labor cost
~$142.7K modeled annual savings
α = 0.75 exponential smoothing · ~6.3% MAPE

The optimization considered drivers, trainees, hiring, training, wages, productivity, and mileage demand while keeping service constraints satisfied. Forecasts based on 2020–2025 freight-weight history informed 2026–2030 staffing scenarios.

Scope & limitations

Modeled result; not represented as realized company savings.

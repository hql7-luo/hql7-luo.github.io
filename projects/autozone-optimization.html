AutoZone Workforce Optimization & Demand Forecasting

Built an Excel Solver workforce model under mileage-demand and service constraints, then used demand forecasts to support staffing and capacity recommendations.

My role

Project team member

Focus

Optimization & forecasting · Sep–Dec 2025

At a glanceFrom problem to system.Evidence & outcomes
Problem

The question worth solving.

How can staffing and training decisions meet freight demand while reducing modeled workforce cost?

System

From problem to system.

Translate freight demand into capacity requirements → optimize hiring and training under service constraints → inform future staffing scenarios with forecasts.

Technologies & methods

Excel Solver / Workforce optimization / Exponential smoothing

Evidence & outcomes

Model and decision

~15% lower modeled annual labor cost
Estimated annual labor savings: ~$142.7K
Exponential smoothing · α = 0.75 · MAPE = 6.3%

The Excel Solver model considered drivers, trainees, hiring, training, wages, productivity, and mileage demand while meeting service constraints. Exponential smoothing of 2020–2025 freight history produced 2026–2030 forecasts to inform staffing and capacity recommendations.

Scope & limitations

Savings are estimates from the model, rather than realized company results.

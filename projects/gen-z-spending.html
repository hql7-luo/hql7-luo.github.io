Business problem

The question worth solving.

Which spending patterns are useful for commercial decisions, beyond income alone?

Approach

From problem to system.

Analyze income, spending, payment and purchase behavior → compare segments → translate findings into product, promotion and fulfillment recommendations.

  1. Analyze income, spending, payment and purchase behavior
  2. Compare segments
  3. Translate findings into product, promotion and fulfillment recommendations

Findings & recommendations

Business recommendations

1,700 Gen Z records · 15 attributes
Income vs spending correlation: r = 0.03
50.6% of sampled records spent above income

Offer affordable but meaningful options, emphasize style and emotional expression, time promotions to bi-weekly and seasonal cadence, and balance convenience with affordable delivery. Higher income alone should not be treated as a reliable signal of higher spending in this sample.

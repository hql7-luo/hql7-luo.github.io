Problem

Turn scattered clues into a clear next step.

A company name or business card does not establish buyer demand. Qualification needs a consistent way to separate evidence, product fit, purchase signals and unanswered questions.

Input: company name, website, business card, inquiry and notes. Output: a customer assessment, 0–100 score, A/B/C/D grade, priority action and three consistent report formats.

User workflow

Business rules that stay traceable.

I organized nine investigation areas, country-adapted evidence rules, printing-product matching and an auditable five-dimension score with risk deductions. Pydantic validation enforces evidence-based grade caps; one report object feeds DOCX, XLSX and JSON, plus deterministic batch ranking.

Output

One result, with its reasons.

The existing fictional Aurora fixture scores 97/100 and derives grade A. The same result card shows customer type, purchase signals, product fit, payment uncertainty, next action and every scoring dimension. It is a generated summary of the report data, not a web-app mockup.

Technical implementation

Technologies & methods

Python · Pydantic · Business logic · Evidence analysis · Workflow design · Document automation

Scope & limitations

Reusable Agent Skill and local report generators, with no standalone public web demo. Public examples are entirely fictional. Missing data stays explicit; research supports commercial judgment and does not replace formal legal, credit or compliance review.

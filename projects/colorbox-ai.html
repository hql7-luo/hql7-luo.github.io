ColorBox AI

A production-focused AI workflow that turns customer packaging files into structured specifications, flags missing information and production risks, and generates standardized review sheets for human approval.

Problem

The question worth solving.

Packaging orders often arrive as unstructured PDFs, spreadsheets, images, Word files, emails, or text, leaving production-critical specifications scattered and easy to miss.

System

From problem to system.

Customer files → AI extraction → deterministic validation → human confirmation → production review sheet.

Bilingual workflow & outputs

AI suggestions remain editable, unrecognized values stay empty, and Zod-validated output is checked again by deterministic factory rules before a person approves the result. The public demo uses fictional data with uploads and database writes disabled; local factory mode supports local files, optional OpenAI-compatible extraction, SQLite history, Excel export, and print/PDF.

Scope & limitations

The public demo uses fictional data and disables uploads and database writes; no customer adoption or realized efficiency gain is claimed.
